Preprints

  • Values of equivariant zeta functions of varieties over finite fields. [Draft]
    Abstract For a finite G-Galois cover of varieties over a finite field of characteristic p, we formulate a K-theoretic leading term formula, which relates the value of the equivariant zeta function at any non-negative integer to the Galois structure of arithmetic and derived de Rham cohomology. This provides an equivariant refinement of Geisser’s result, as well as the arithmetic cohomology version of the main result of Burns–Kakde. We show that the l-part of the formula is valid for all primes l different from p, and that the p-part is valid modulo the torsion subgroup of K_0(Z_p[G], Q_p[G]).

  • Non-induced representations of finite cyclic groups, 2021. [arXiv, not intended for publication.]
    Abstract Let K be an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0 and let G be a finite cyclic group of order n. In this note we prove, using induction on the number of prime divisors of n, that R_K(G)/I is isomorphic to Z[X]/⟨Phi_n(X)⟩ where R_K(G) denotes the ring of K-representations of G and I is the sum of ideals Ind_H^G(R_K(H)) of R_K(G) as H varies over all proper subgroups of G. This gives us an idea of how many representations of G are not induced from representations of a proper subgroup.

In preparation

  • Equivariant special value at s = 1/2.
    Overview We prove a K-theoretic formula for the special value at s = 1/2, of the equivariant zeta function of varieties over finite fields in terms of Galois structure of Weil-eh cohomology with coefficients in a supersingular elliptic curve. This generalizes the work of Ramachandran.
